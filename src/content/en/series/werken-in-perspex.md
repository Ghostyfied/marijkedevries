**The layers of reality**

The Perspex series originated on the occasion of an artist-in-residency in 2015 in Spain, following a commission from a Spanish gallery. Working in mixed techniques between and on the layers of perspex brings richness and variety to the use of material.

The technique expresses De Vries's themes of change, transparency and layeredness.

The work relates to time through its theme of constant change. Reality as we perceive it is not static; we cannot experience 'all there is' in time. This dynamic of change and transformation is present in the perspex works. Theme and style refer to a layered world of meaning that is always coming into being and always changing. A premonition of the not-yet visible, a reminiscence of the almost dissolved, already leaving the picture plane (and sometimes becoming sculptural).

**Layeredness — technique and meaning**

In a period of my life I was invited to make work by a silkscreen printer. What fascinated and touched me in working with that 'simple' technique is that, through the clear superimposition of form and colour, the earlier reality or state of the work is transformed while still shimmering through. With the unruliness of paint, such an effect is much harder to achieve. It is something I look for when I paint. And in working with the layering of perspex. The different states and layers that life adds, as it were, through which an earlier core shimmers. It evokes a nostalgia of 'being underway', leaving its traces.
