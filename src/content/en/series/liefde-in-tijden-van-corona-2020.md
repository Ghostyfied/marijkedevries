How is love doing in times of Corona, when you are single and dating in the 1.5-metre society?

We must avoid each other, yet we are looking for love. Human contact has changed; we keep our distance as much as we can. Skin hunger, caution. Every stranger is a possible danger.

Meanwhile I am working on 'Tinder Times', about online dating. The project has several layers and angles. It consists of a programme around an exhibition of my work, opening (postponed by Corona) in November 2020, with thanks to the AFK.

One part of the project consists of painting Tinder dates. Painting live is of course no longer possible. I paint dates through online sessions. This turn changes the perspective: that of my iPhone.

Love nestles in stories of the soul that lead to layered works about dreams, yearnings, separation anxiety and memories.

The work 'Liefde in tijden van Corona' is one of the works from the series of the same name. The world seems to be falling apart into pieces. A figure moves in free fall through a fragmented universe. Yet this movement also expresses a synthesis, in which the pieces come together into a new image.
