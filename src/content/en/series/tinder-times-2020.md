In the project Tinder Times the artist combines a classic medium — she works primarily from painting — with a contemporary phenomenon: dating through a digital platform such as Tinder. 'Tinder Times' is an artistic investigation into online dating in which De Vries engages with current social themes such as transience, sexual identity, role patterns and changing forms of relationships, touching on contemporary feminism as well. At the same time it is a personal investigation in which the date is the occasion for the artwork. Making art about this subject is her artistic response to the transience of the virtual world, and a way to transform it.

**Curator Fleur Feringa**: In her project Tinder Times, Marijke de Vries opens a taboo-breaking conversation about sexuality in an online era. She breaks through role patterns and challenges the viewer to let go of shame and judgement. Marijke de Vries connects the digital dating world, so often aimed at brief contact, to a universal longing for intimacy. The online and offline encounters Marijke had in the course of Tinder Times have resulted in a series of model drawings and more abstract multimedia works. Longing, tension and fleeting contact are made tangible in a tender way. The artworks and the exhibition at Sexyland form a starting point for dialogue about a subject that touches everyone: sexuality and connection. In the exhibition we deliberately seek out a voyeuristic character in the presentation, to give visitors a sense of participation. In the surrounding programme we reverse the roles and ask visitors and participants about their own experiences.
